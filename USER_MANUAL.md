## ✋ HAND GESTURES (Spatial Mathematics)

T.O.M.M.Y. tracks 21 points on your hand to identify these "Macro Shapes":

### 1. The Wand (Cursor & Edit)
*   **Shape:** Hold up just your **Index Finger**.
*   **Action:** Moves the cursor.
*   **Tip:** Pinch your **Index + Thumb** to click and drag.

### 2. The Scissors (Scroll)
*   **Shape:** Hold up **Index + Middle** fingers (Peace Sign).
*   **Action:** Move your hand up/down to scroll. Pinch the fingers together to scroll faster.

### 3. The Power Palm (Context Swipe)
*   **Shape:** Open your **Full Hand (5 fingers)**.
*   **Action:** Swipe Left/Right to trigger app-specific actions.
    *   🌍 **Browsers:** Switch Tabs.
    *   📝 **Editors:** Undo/Redo.
    *   🎵 **Media:** Skip Tracks.

### 4. The Hardware Overlord (Shaka)
*   **Shape:** Extend only **Thumb + Pinky** (Hang Loose).
*   **Action:** Control System Volume and Brightness.

### 5. The Confirmation (Thumbs Up)
*   **Shape:** Give a **Thumbs Up 👍**.
*   **Action:** Press **ENTER**. Use this for confirming dialogs.

## 👁️ EYE CONTROL (Optic Protocols)

When you close both hands, T.O.M.M.Y. shifts to **Ocular Tracking Mode**.

### 1. Navigation
*   **Nose Tracking:** Move your nose to guide the cursor. The system automatically corrects for your posture.

### 2. Left/Right Click
*   **Quick Blink:** Click.
*   **Double Blink:** Right Click.

### 3. The Simple Confirm
*   **Long Blink (0.5s):** Press **ENTER**.

### 4. Writing Mode (Dictation)
*   **Mouth Open (Hold):** While in Eye Mode, keep your mouth open to start typing whatever you speak.
